# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AI Plays Pokémon to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for contributors, users, researchers, and maintainers of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience, respect, and good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome different perspectives and ask clarifying questions before assuming intent;
- give and accept actionable feedback;
- protect private information, credentials, and copyrighted ROM data;
- follow the project's security and contribution guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not tolerated:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized, hateful, or degrading language directed at another participant;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption, impersonation, spam, or bad-faith reporting;
- sharing credentials, API keys, private logs, or ROM files in public project channels;
- any other conduct that would make participation unsafe or unreasonably hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to protect the community. Maintainers will seek to apply this policy consistently and proportionately.

## Reporting a concern

For routine conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the project's GitHub repository. For security vulnerabilities, do not use a public issue; follow the private-reporting instructions in `SECURITY.md`.

Reports should include the relevant links or excerpts, dates, participants, and any context needed to investigate. Please avoid forwarding private information beyond what is necessary.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community standards and tailored to this project's security and ROM-handling concerns.
