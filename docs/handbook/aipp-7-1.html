FLEET BUILD-AND-VERIFY
7.1.1

INTRODUCTION.

The fleet builds and verifies the agent: a scheduler admits a foreman by board work, the foreman dispatches one worker per tick, a judge grades the evidence, and the board remembers. Nothing lands on a claim (figure 7.1-1).

7.1.2

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION.

The lane's admission mode is tasks: a non-empty board waives the six-hour cooldown pin. Workers run in worktrees with their own environments; guards run at commit; releases are cut only on owner authorisation. Verification receipts and verdicts land beside the code they grade.

7.1.2.1

Idle Spin, Measured.

Drained must mean no WORKABLE row. It currently means no open row: blocked and dep-gated rows waived the cooldown and produced 42 dry ticks in six hours on this lane alone - 8.3 million input tokens for nothing.

7.1.2.2

What A Good Tick Is.

A tick that reports nothing changed, honestly, in one line, is a good tick. A tick that re-audits finished work is a wasted one. The foreman prompt says so; the admission loop must not fight it by re-admitting every three minutes.

7.1.2.3

The Judge.

Tier 1 runs secrets, static analysis and tests; tier 2 grades the evidence. Judge runs have been refused with a 402 billing fault, and a gate whose judge never sat is recorded DEGRADED, never PASS.
