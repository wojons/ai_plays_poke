4.1.1

INTRODUCTION.

The vision channel describes the screen the way the RAM reader describes the room: one character per cell. It is the only channel that can name what a thing is - a person, a doorway, grass - and the only channel that hallucinates (figure 4.1-1).

4.1.2

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION.

The frame is enlarged six times with LANCZOS smoothing, capped at 1024 pixels, and sent to the configured model with a terse grid prompt. The reply is parsed through code fences, clamped to nine rows of ten characters, and compared against the RAM grid. Model, provider and prompt are measured variables, not tastes.

4.1.2.1

Models Are Multi-Possible.

AIPP_VISION_MODELS carries an ordered list; the default served model is GPT-4O-MINI at 2.4 seconds and 25k tokens. KIMI K3, 2.8 trillion parameters, measured 62.2 percent at 97 seconds - bigger and newer was worse.

4.1.2.2

People Are A Different Task.

The people probe scored exact sprite cells against wSpriteStateData1: one frame in three correct, and the model answered girl at cell 1,4 on all three frames including the two where no girl stands there. It anchors. Constrain the question to RAM-predicted cells or do not trust the answer.

4.1.2.3

Cost Discipline.

A reading costs about 25,000 input tokens. The auto trigger reads on movement or scene change only; the 120-second idle timer was removed on 2026-10-01 after it generated readings around the clock for nothing.
