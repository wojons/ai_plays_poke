5. Memory Leak Finding (GAMEPLAY-LEAK-001, P0)

🚨 Battle-phase native leak — ~70–100 MB/s

Symptoms: RSS grows linearly from the first real battle cycle; process dies at ~50 GB (~9 min). Output truncates mid-line — instant death, no Python traceback.

Evidence chain:

  • mem-monitor: avail 49 G → 4.3 G while a venv/bin/python held 48–50 GB; freed within 30 s of death. cgroup oom 0 — not cgroup OOM.
  • tracemalloc in-process: Python heap 10 MB flat through battle start — not a Python-object leak.
  • Pure-emulator bisect: 24,000 ticks + captures from 5 checkpoints — 66–72 MB flat.
  • Control (run 5, no battle loop): 134 MB flat for 10+ min, 23 Luna calls.
  • All leaking runs share: battle StateWindow loop (12× deepseek-v4-flash + battle tools + wait(60)+fast_forward(180) spans).

Ruled out: Python heap, frame cache, state_window history, OpenRouterClient, plain ticking.

Prime suspects: (a) pyboy native battle rendering at fast-forward; (b) SDL2 audio queue on SGB ROM; (c) RAM-read × pyboy memory-view interaction.

Next: worker task — py-spy/smaps during live battle, bisect pyboy/SDL2/SGB, fix + regression test. Until fixed, Route-1 runs die at ~9 min.

Secondary: HSM rejects BOOT.INITIALIZE → BATTLE.BATTLE_MENU and spams "Invalid transition attempted" in a loop when battle detection races the HSM (run 5) — folded into GAMEPLAY-LEAK-001 scope.
